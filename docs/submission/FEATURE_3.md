# Feature 3: Reserve & Unlock in the App

Purpose: the rider finds the nearest bike, reserves it for 15 minutes with a walking route to it, and opens its smart lock from the app (the lock only opens through the app), which starts the ride and the live meter.

Test: tick the two small boxes, tap Skip, tap "Reserve · 0.250 KD", pick Apple Pay, tap "Walk to the bike" or "I'm here", then "Unlock in the app".

Pictures: docs/submission/feature3-all-3.png (1 nearest bike, 2 reserved with hold timer and route, 3 ride started with the meter).
